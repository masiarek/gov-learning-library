## DECLARING INDEPENDENCE — PRE REVOLUTION

**PRE REVOLUTION: TENSIONS RISE.** Tensions build between the British and American colonists following the French and Indian war.

Results of the French and Indian war:

- England beats France
- England has a great amount of land in America
- Has high debt from fighting an overseas war, needs colonies to pay some of it.

## THE FINAL PUSH

**FIRST CONTINENTAL CONGRESS**

- a meeting of 56 delegates from 12 of 13 ~~states~~ colonies (all except Georgia) held in response to the British Parliament's Intolerable Acts.
- Attended by prominent figures like George Washington, Patrick Henry, John Adams, and Samuel Adams.
- **DECLARATION OF RIGHTS:** document adopted by the FCC to protest unfair British policies. Established a trade boycott against Great Britain.

**COMMON SENSE**

- Pamphlet written by Thomas Payne&nbsp;[⚠](../../CORRECTIONS.md#5-thomas-paine)
- Called for independence and formation of a democratic republic.

## DECLARING INDEPENDENCE

- State legislatures elect delegates to send to the 2nd Continental Congress
- Delegates approve the resolution to declare the 13 colonies as independent.
- Delegates sign a **petition** to King George III and parliament, officially declaring independence July 4, 1776.&nbsp;[⚠](../../CORRECTIONS.md#1-the-declaration-was-not-a-petition)

## DECLARATION OF INDEPENDENCE

A notice to the king that the colonies declare themselves independent.

- mostly a list of grievances.
- was not meant to be private.
    - Made public so that other countries could see and support.
    - Major supporter was France.
- Claimed that the government / king was ignoring natural rights. Everyone was MAD AS HELL!!!
- Based on Lockian [*Lockean*] ideas of a Republican form of government based on the consent of the governed, through a social contract.
- A framework to understand the principles underlying American gov.

## Corrections on this page

| ⚠ | The note | Actually |
|---|---|---|
| [1](../../CORRECTIONS.md#1-the-declaration-was-not-a-petition) | "Delegates sign a **petition** … July 4, 1776" | Not a petition — that was the **Olive Branch Petition** (July 1775). And it was **adopted** July 4, **signed** August 2. |
| [2](../../CORRECTIONS.md#2-the-tea-party-was-for-no-taxation-without-representation-not-against-it) | Tea Party "**against** the SoL idea" | The Sons of Liberty *organised* it. It was **in defence of** "no taxation without representation". |
| [5](../../CORRECTIONS.md#5-thomas-paine) | "Thomas **Payne**" | **Thomas Paine.** |

Also, minor: **State legislatures** were still **colonial assemblies and provincial congresses** in 1775, and *Lockian* is spelled **Lockean**.

**Correct, in case of doubt:** 5 dead / 6 wounded at the Massacre ✓ · 56 delegates from 12 of 13 colonies, Georgia absent ✓ · France as the major foreign supporter ✓ · "mostly a list of grievances" ✓.
