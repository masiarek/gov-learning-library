## DEMOCRACY REVIEW

**DEMOCRACY** is a form of government in which the people make the decisions, whether meeting directly or by elected representatives.

**DIRECT (PURE) DEMOCRACY** is a form of government when the people *are* the government. Every decision is voted on by the people. Incredibly ineffective, as it would take very long to make decisions about minute matters.

**REPRESENTATIVE DEMOCRACY:** A small group of officials elected to represent the people. United States is a representative democracy.

> ex: **REFERENDUM** or **BALLOT INITIATIVE**, which is the practice of submitting a law to a popular vote at election time. The law may be proposed by a voter's initiative or by the legislature.&nbsp;[⚠](../../CORRECTIONS.md#4-referendum-and-initiative-are-direct-democracy)

## THEORIES OF DEMOCRACY

Three main models (theories) of democracy that explain how power and influence operate in the U.S.

**1. PARTICIPATORY DEMOCRACY**

- emphasizes direct citizen involvement in politics and civil society
- citizens vote directly on laws & policies rather than relying on intermediaries.
- ex: Town hall meetings, ballot initiatives, protests, voting, funding causes
- emphasizes broad participation in politics and civil society

**2. PLURALIST DEMOCRACY**

- Recognizes group-based activism by non-governmental interests striving for an impact on political decisions.
- The idea that individually, it's more difficult to influence, and so it's beneficial to make private, non-governmental groups to do so.
- ex: Interest groups, labor unions.

**3. ELITE DEMOCRACY**

- Emphasizes limited citizen participation in politics and civil society.
- A small number of people, usually wealthy and well educated, have the most power.
- Prior to the 17th amendment, the senate was believed to have the most power — they were educated, wealthy white men who weren't elected by the people.

## IDEALS OF DEMOCRACY

**LIMITED GOVERNMENT, NATURAL RIGHTS, POPULAR SOVEREIGNTY, REPUBLICANISM, & SOCIAL CONTRACT** are core ideals of American democracy, which come from Enlightenment thinkers and shape our foundational documents.

**LIMITED GOVERNMENT**

- A government's power over its citizens has restrictions.
- Governmental authority is prescribed, restricted, and limited by law or by the constitution.

**NATURAL RIGHTS**

- There are rights important for all humans **and animals** to have out of natural law.&nbsp;[⚠](../../CORRECTIONS.md#3-natural-rights-are-human-rights)
- Life, liberty, and pursuit of happiness (previously property).

**POPULAR SOVEREIGNTY**

- A doctrine in political theory that the government is created by and is subject to the will of its people — the power is in the hands of the people.

**REPUBLICANISM**

- Adherence to a republican government, which is to say, the people's beliefs are expressed through elected officials.

**SOCIAL CONTRACT**

- an implicit agreement among the members of a society to cooperate for social benefits.
- members of a society pay taxes, follow the laws, serve in the military, etc, in return for public benefits like schools, libraries, armies, etc. functioning, safe society.
- Give up something, get something else in return.
