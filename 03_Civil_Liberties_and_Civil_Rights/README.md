**In one line:** What government may not do to you (liberties), and what it must not let others do to you (rights).

The distinction to hold from the start: **civil liberties** are protections *from* government action — mostly in the Bill of Rights. **Civil rights** are protections *by* government against discrimination — mostly through the Fourteenth Amendment and statute.
